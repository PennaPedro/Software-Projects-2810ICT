## Review Notes

The Sprint 2 artefacts were checked against the current XPower requirements and the project structure.

The team now has:

- a consolidated set of functional and non-functional requirements;
- identified project risks and measurable quality metrics;
- a tariff configuration model for Flat Rate, Time-of-Use and Tiered tariffs;
- a system-level Use Case Diagram and five detailed Use Case Descriptions;
